## Brand & Style
This design system sets the standard for a calm, authoritative, and motivating financial wellness interface. Moving away from standard sterile corporate fintech patterns, the design language couples deep forest botanical greens with radiant pistachio accents and subtle, celebratory warm golds. The interface conveys deliberate control, long-term wealth composure, and intuitive financial clarity.

The visual direction merges **refined modernism with subtle tactile and glass accents**:
- **Structured and Clean:** Systematic 12-column frameworks, crisp micro-borders, and disciplined alignments inspired by elite productivity tools.
- **Organic Precision:** Generous white space balanced by soft, tinted surfaces that avoid sterile coldness.
- **Asymmetric Tension:** Dynamic card balances, intentional metric scaling, and offset status badges to emphasize crucial financial inflection points without cognitive overload.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on high contrast between deep forest hues and glowing, verdant highlights to convey financial security and growth.

### Palette Application
- **Primary (`#174D42`) & Dark Surface Container (`#103C34`):** Anchors primary calls-to-action, active sidebar states, dense metric hubs, and core navigational headers. High contrast against light foundations.
- **Secondary / Pistachio Accent (`#B8E4A5`):** Used deliberately for progression bars, forward-moving financial metrics, interactive toggles, and high-priority indicators. Paired with deep emerald typography for legibility.
- **Tertiary / Warm Gold (`#F3D9A4`):** Reserved for financial achievements, milestone badges, yield gains, and vault unlock states. Never overused as a base brand fill.
- **Surfaces & Canvas:**
  - Base Canvas: `#F6F8F1` (warm, natural linen off-white).
  - Elevated Container: `#FFFFFF` (crisp card decks and modals).
  - Subdued Panel: `#EDF3E9` (recessed track inputs, tabular headers, secondary metrics).
  - Soft Tag Tint: `#E6F3DD` (badge backdrops, subtle card highlights).
- **Typography & Structure:**
  - Primary Content: `#1C302A` (deep slate emerald ensuring AAA accessibility on light surfaces).
  - Secondary Content: `#64756D` (slate-green supportive labels, subheadings, and timestamps).
  - Structural Hairlines: `#DFE8DC` (subtle partition lines and table rows).
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - Growth / Deposit: `#27865D`
  - Warning / Caution: `#C58A2B`
  - Expense / Drift: `#D66B63`

## Typography
The system enforces a dual-type architecture:
- **Headlines, Metric Displays, and Labels:** Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Its sculpted geometry, geometric roundness, and tight letter spacing deliver authority to monetary balances and dashboard sectioning. Tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be activated across all metric levels to prevent shift during streaming financial updates.
- **Body & Tabular Data:** Set in **Inter** for maximum clarity across ledger lists, micro-copy, and dense transactional feeds.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout system is founded on a 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous horizontal boundaries to maintain an expansive, luxury feel.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Reflow
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column layout; `2.5rem` outer margins; `1.5rem` gutters. Accommodates multi-tier dashboards featuring persistent sidebar navigation, primary metric grids, and asymmetric contextual drawers.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout; `1.5rem` outer margins; `1rem` gutters. Collapses the primary navigation to a compact rail; secondary metrics stack into a balanced 2x2 modular matrix.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout; `1.25rem` outer canvas padding; `0.75rem` gutters. Stacks asymmetric desktop card arrangements into single vertical flows; key metric components maintain edge-to-edge full width with internal padding.

### Spacing Philosophy
Rhythm is dictated by a strict 4px/8px base factor. Layout blocks favor loose interior breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) inside cards to contrast with compact, scannable data density in tabular transaction ledgers (`space-xs` and `space-sm`).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows, relying instead on **chroma-tinted ambient layers, crisp hairlines, and translucent surface containers**.

### Hierarchy of Surfaces
1. **Canvas (Base Level):** Grounded in `#F6F8F1`. Flat, non-reflective base plane.
2. **Subdued Containers (Level 0):** `#EDF3E9` surfaces nested directly onto the canvas for segmented navigation, input boxes, and auxiliary summary zones. Contained with a 1px border of `#DFE8DC`.
3. **Elevated Primary Decks (Level 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` surfaces featuring a 1px stroke of `#DFE8DC` complemented by an ultra-soft botanical ambient shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(23, 77, 66, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(16, 60, 52, 0.03);`
4. **Interactive Overlays & Flyouts (Level 2):** Modals, drawer navigations, and floating balance pills leverage high-altitude separation with slight green-tinted depth:
   `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -12px rgba(16, 60, 52, 0.12), 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(23, 77, 66, 0.06);`
5. **Glass Frost Elements:** Header banners and sticky transaction filters use `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `rgba(246, 248, 241, 0.85)` framed with a bottom borderline of `#DFE8DC`.

## Shapes
A baseline roundedness of `2` provides a polished, approachable structural tone without sacrificing fintech discipline:
- **Base Components (`rounded`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Input elements, transactional rows, dropdown menus, and standard card internal blocks.
- **Primary Content Decks (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Metric widgets, analytical charts, and modal dialogs.
- **Feature Cards & Hero Modules (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Primary wealth summary anchors and promotional visual headers.
- **Interactive Badges & Pills (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Status badges, category labels, numeric indicators, and toggle selectors maintain clean, complete pill borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Emerald background (`#174D42`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-lg` (8px). Subtle border: `1px solid #103C34`. On hover: transition to `#103C34` with an ambient glow of `rgba(23, 77, 66, 0.15)`.
- **Secondary / Accent:** Pistachio Green (`#B8E4A5`), deep emerald text (`#103C34`), font weight 600. Active states scale down to `0.98` with crisp immediate feedback.
- **Subtle / Ghost:** Transparent background, slate emerald text (`#1C302A`), hover background in `#EDF3E9`.
- **Destructive:** Light coral background with rich coral text (`#D66B63`), transitioning to solid coral with white typography on active confirmation.

### Chips & Badges
- Encapsulated in full-radius pill styling (`rounded-full`).
- **Growth / Positive:** Background `#E6F3DD`, Text `#27865D`, 1px border in `rgba(39, 134, 93, 0.2)`.
- **Milestone / Savings:** Background `#FDF6E7`, Text `#A86F15`, 1px border in `#F3D9A4`.
- **Neutral / Category:** Background `#EDF3E9`, Text `#64756D`.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Height: 44px for high-touch targets.
- Base style: Surface `#EDF3E9`, border `1px solid #DFE8DC`, typography `Inter 14px` (`#1C302A`).
- Focus state: Surface turns to `#FFFFFF`, border transitions to Deep Emerald (`#174D42`) accompanied by a 3px ring of `rgba(184, 228, 165, 0.4)`.
- Error state: Border `#D66B63` accompanied by a light coral ring.

### Cards & Modular Decks
- Standard layout utilizes asymmetric visual anchors: a solid white deck accented with a top-right pistachio micro-chip or a muted warm gold milestone marker.
- Padding: `1.5rem` (`space-lg`) on mobile, `2rem` on desktop.
- Structural partitions within cards use `1px solid #DFE8DC`.

### Transaction Lists
- Row layout uses horizontal flex distribution with `space-sm` vertical spacing.
- Left-aligned icon wrapper: 40px circular container (`#EDF3E9` or `#E6F3DD`) with centered deep emerald icons.
- Right-aligned currency metrics: `Plus Jakarta Sans` 14px bold, with positive additions prefixed by a Pistachio/Emerald indicator and subtractions displayed in `#1C302A`.
- Hover state: Entire row subtly shifts to `#F0F5ED` with a smooth 150ms transition.

### Checkboxes, Switches & Radio Controls
- Checkboxes & Radios: Inactive border `1.5px solid #DFE8DC`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active state fills with `#174D42` displaying crisp white glyphs.
- Toggle Switches: Track background `#EDF3E9` (inactive) swapping to `#174D42` (active); internal thumb uses Pistachio Green (`#B8E4A5`) for an unexpected, high-end pop of identity.

### Financial Health Bar (Specialty Component)
- Track: 8px tall, `rounded-full`, background `#EDF3E9`.
- Filled segment: Linear gradient from `#174D42` to `#B8E4A5` with smooth CSS width transitions. Overdraft thresholds turn immediately to `#D66B63`.
